# Experiments queued after the foundation gate

These are hypotheses, not results. No storage or differentiation architecture is
promoted before its discriminating cases and required laws pass.

## Storage and dependent certificates

Compare a small dependent list, a reusable immutable balanced tree, and an owned
padded array. Construct lengths 0, 1, 3, 4, and 5; read twice; update; clone;
read both owners; map and compute a dot product. Prove logical get/set/frame
laws for the selected representation, and connect them to its executable read
and update functions. First establish that an erased certificate and owned
storage pass both checkers without unsafe definitions. Reject raw wrapping
indices before access. Measure native time and allocation separately from
checking and compilation. Stop promotion if a representation needs unsafe
sharing, cannot express its invariant, or has pathological copying.

## Repeated-input differentiation

Compare a one-shot affine closure pullback, a tagged reverse tape, and forward
dual numbers on a scalar polynomial, `x*x`, a diamond graph, and a tiny affine
layer. Record saved-value counts, cloning, gradient contributions, memory, and
both-checker results. The mathematical interpretation and derivative must be
defined independently from the transformation. Backward consumes its graph.
Missing repeated-input contributions or unexplained unsafe assumptions block
promotion. Retained graphs and higher-order differentiation are excluded.
